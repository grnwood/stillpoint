Knowledge IDE

Your notes are the source. StillPoint is the workbench around them.

A Knowledge IDE brings the tools of serious knowledge work into one coherent place: writing, navigation, structure, relationships, visualization, automation, and agents. StillPoint keeps all of that optional. If you only want a fast, calm notes app, that is exactly what it remains.

One integrated workbench

The page stays at the center.

Find and jump

Search locally, switch through recent pages, browse the vault in a quick picker, jump to a heading, or run a command without breaking your train of thought.

Read structure and context

The headings outline shows the shape inside a page. Backlinks and the Link Navigator show how that page participates in the wider vault.

Act without separating context

Tasks, dates, diagrams, attachments, and AI conversations stay close to the notes that explain them instead of becoming disconnected records in another system.

Take the vault with you

Homebase can synchronize local copies across devices, while the mobile PWA keeps notes viewable and editable on the go.

Your notes remain plain text, portable without format lock-in or conversion.

Use another view when words are not enough

Mindmaps expose document hierarchy. The graph and Link Navigator expose relationships. Focus and Audience modes change the presentation without changing the underlying Markdown.

Navigation should feel like part of thinking.

Use quick page jumping, recent-page switching, heading pickers, command-palette actions, and keyboard-first panels to move from thought to thought without hunting through menus. The same workspace can stay quiet for writing or open outward for deeper exploration.

Power is only useful when it stays invisible until you need it.
