Mindmap Mode

See your markdown as a living structure.

StillPoint can visualize your page as a mindmap so you can read, think, and organize from a visual angle. It is the same page, the same markdown, and the same source of truth, presented in a way that helps structure click faster.

Read and Understand Faster

Visual reading for complex documents.

Long pages are easier to scan when section shape is visible at a glance. Mindmap Mode lets you move through headings and branches without losing context, then jump directly back to the exact paragraph in markdown when needed.

Brainstorm, craft notes, and reshape ideas.

This is not just for viewing. You can use the map to manipulate page structure while you author: move nodes, regroup sections, and keep refining until the page matches your thinking. Flip between visual structure and document writing fluidly.

Build structure visually, then polish in markdown. StillPoint keeps both in sync.

Focus

Filter hierarchy to work on what matters now.

When a page gets large, narrow the hierarchy to just the branch you care about. Focused filtering lets you work deeply on one thread without losing the larger page architecture.

How to Open It

Start from where you already work.

From the editor

Right-click in the editor and open View -> View in Map Mode.

From the status bar

Click the mindmap icon in the status bar: Open in Mindmap mode.

From the right panel

Use the Map tab for side-by-side authoring while you keep writing in the editor.
