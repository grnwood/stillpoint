# Welcome to StillPoint  
---

![sp-full-transparent-small](./sp-full-transparent-small.png)

StillPoint is a local-first workspace for notes, plans, tasks, and references. Your work lives in Markdown files inside a vault folder you control, so you can organize it any way you want, link pages together, and keep everything searchable.

If you are new, think of StillPoint in two common modes:

- **Personal brain**
    Use one vault as your daily system for notes, planning, and logging.
      - Build any folder/page structure you want.
      - Use the journal as a daily planner and daily log.
      - `Alt+D` jumps to Today, or use the Home button.
- **Project vaults**
    Use separate vaults for focused work like classes, writing, or client projects.
      - Keep each project isolated, with its own pages and links.
      - Use Tasks/Calendar if useful, or just use it as a clean writing and linking workspace.

StillPoint helps answer practical questions quickly:
- What did I do today? (Journal, Tasks)
- What do I still need to do on this project? (Tasks, tags)
- What is due this week? (Journal, due dates)
- Where is that page I made? (`Ctrl+J` jump to page, `Ctrl+Shift+P` search)
- How are ideas connected? (Link graph and backlinks)
- Advanced: ask AI to search your vault and draft summaries

## How to Use This Help Vault
- Each topic is a page (folder/page).
- Click pages in the left navigation to explore.
- `Alt+PgUp` and `Alt+PgDown` cycle through pages in the left nav.

## Quick Tour
- Vaults are folders of pages stored on disk.
- Pages are Markdown files organized as `Page/Page.md`.
- Left sidebar: Vault tree, Tags, Search.
- Center: the editor.
- Right sidebar: Tasks, Calendar, Attachments, Link Navigator, AI.

You can enable or disable most panels in Preferences.
